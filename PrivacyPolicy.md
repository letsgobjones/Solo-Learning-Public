# Privacy Policy for Solo Learning

**Last Updated:** October 23, 2026

**Introduction**

Thank you for using Solo Learning! This policy explains how Brandon Jones ("we," "us") handles your information when you use our mobile app (the "Application").

**In short:** Solo Learning has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress and settings stay on your device, and we do not receive them.

**Information Stored on Your Device**

The Application keeps the following on your device so the app can work:

*   Your hero's name and appearance.
*   Your learning progress: which words you have answered, your review schedule, your level, coins, items, achievements and streak.
*   Your settings, such as sound, voice, quiz direction and the reminder time.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is kept for as long as the Application remains installed, and deleting the Application deletes it.

**Information We Collect**

We do not collect any personal information through the Application, and we do not run a server that receives your data.

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us, so we can reply.

We do *not* collect precise location data, contacts, photos, or use your camera or microphone.

**Internet Requests**

The Application makes one kind of internet request: after a defeat, it can ask the ZenQuotes service ([zenquotes.io](https://zenquotes.io/)) for an inspirational quote. We send no personal information or identifier with that request. As with any request on the internet, the service can see your device's IP address, and it handles that under its own privacy practices. If no connection is available, the Application shows a Korean phrase instead.

**Notifications**

The daily reminder is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, the Application asks iOS for permission and schedules a notification on your device. The reminder is created and delivered by your device, not by a server. You can change it or turn it off in the Application (Profile > Reminder), or turn off notifications for the Application in iOS Settings.

**Sharing**

If you tap "Share my progress," the Application creates a picture of your level, streak and words learned on your device. It goes only where you choose to send it through the iOS share sheet.

**Information From Apple**

Apple may give us aggregated information about the Application, such as downloads and crash reports, from users who agreed to share it with developers in their device settings. It does not identify you.

**How We Use Information**

*   To respond to support requests.
*   To understand and fix problems you report.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

**Third-Party Access**

We do not share your information with third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

**Opt-Out Rights**

Uninstall the Application to remove everything it has stored on your device.

**Data Retention**

We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you. Contact us at eons.crustal_2o@icloud.com to delete your support-related data.

Data stored on your device is retained as long as the Application remains installed.

**Children's Privacy**

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. You must be the minimum age of consent, as defined by your nation's laws, to submit personal information through the Application.

**Security**

Your information stays on your device, so we encourage you to keep your device safe and protected by a passcode. We take reasonable steps to protect any data we handle, but no method is 100% secure.

**Changes**

We may update this policy, and we will change the date above when we do. If we ever add features that collect information, such as accounts, advertising or analytics, we will update this policy to describe them. Continued use means you accept the changes.

**Your Consent**

By using the Application, you consent to this policy.

**Contact Us**

Contact us at eons.crustal_2o@icloud.com with any questions.
