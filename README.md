# Support for Solo Learning - Your Korean Language Quest Starts Here!

Welcome to the support page for Solo Learning, the RPG-infused app designed to make learning Korean engaging, effective, and fun!

Solo Learning is free, works on iPhone and iPad (iOS 26 or later), and teaches you Korean vocabulary by letting you battle monsters: every right answer lands a hit.

## What's New in 1.2

*   **Gear you can upgrade:** weapons and shields now come in five tiers. Buy a piece in the Shop, then upgrade it with coins in your Pack and watch its art and stats grow.
*   **Hero screen:** see your stats (with what your gear adds) and exactly what you have equipped.
*   **Two Korean voices:** pick **Aoede**, **Charon** or **Mixed** in Profile. Every word has a recorded pronunciation.
*   **Words to practice:** after a battle, the words you missed are listed with audio, and **Practice these** starts a fight that asks only those words.
*   **Listening mode:** hear the word and pick its meaning, with nothing to read.
*   **Daily review goal and streak:** practise 10 words a day to build a streak.
*   **Daily reminder (optional):** a gentle nudge at a time you choose, skipped on days you've already hit your goal.
*   **Loot drops:** monsters can drop boosts, and tougher ones drop gear.
*   **Share my progress:** send a picture of your level, streak and words learned.
*   **Meet Sol-i (솔이):** the white tiger who cheers you on in Camp.
*   **Nine languages:** the app and the word meanings are available in English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) and Vietnamese. The translations are drafts, so please tell me if something reads wrong.

## Getting Started with Solo Learning

Solo Learning is designed to empower you to conquer the Korean language through a unique RPG adventure. If you're new to the app, here are a few steps to get you started on your quest:

*   **Choose Your Hero:** Select your preferred character type and customize their appearance to match your style.
*   **Start Your Hangul Bootcamp (If Needed):** If you're new to Korean, begin with the Hangul pre-lesson to master the foundational building blocks of the language.
*   **Embark on Quests:** Dive into the main learning content, where you'll battle monsters and progress through an exciting storyline by mastering Korean flashcards.
*   **Come Back Daily:** Answer 10 different words a day to hit your goal and keep your streak alive. Words you miss come back sooner, and words you know come back later.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Before reaching out for personal support, please check our FAQ section. You might find the answer to your question, saving you time and helping me focus on building new content and features.

*   **How do I customize my hero?**
    *   You can customize your hero's appearance and color scheme from the character selection or profile screen.
*   **How do I access the Hangul Bootcamp?**
    *   The Hangul Bootcamp is located in the main menu or can be selected when first starting the app. It is designed to take you from knowing nothing about the Korean Alphabet to reading Hangul.
*   **How do I battle monsters?**
    *   During your learning sessions, flashcards will appear. Answering the Korean vocabulary questions correctly will unleash your hero's skills and defeat the monsters!
*   **Is Solo Learning designed for the KIIP program?**
    *   Yes, Solo Learning has content that is designed to align with the Korea Immigration & Integration Program (KIIP).
*   **What do the quiz directions mean?**
    *   In Profile, choose **한 → En** (see the Korean word, pick the meaning), **En → 한** (see the meaning, pick the Korean word, which is harder), **Mixed**, or **🔊 → En** (listening: hear the word, pick the meaning).
*   **How does the app decide which words I see?**
    *   It uses spaced repetition. Words you get wrong, and words that are due for review, come up most often. The **Review** card on the Camp screen starts a battle with only the words that are due.
*   **How do I equip and upgrade gear?**
    *   Buy gear in the **Shop**, then open your **Pack** to equip it. Weapons and shields can be upgraded for coins (the price is on the Upgrade button). Your **Hero** screen, which you open from the profile button in Camp, shows everything you have equipped and what it adds to your stats.
*   **Which Korean voice does the app use?**
    *   Words play a recorded voice. In **Profile**, choose Aoede, Charon or Mixed. If a word has no recording, the app uses the best Korean voice on your device. For a more natural fallback voice, download a Korean Premium or Enhanced voice in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken Content > Voices.
*   **How do I turn on the daily reminder?**
    *   Open **Profile > Reminder** and switch on **Daily reminder**. iOS will ask for permission to send notifications. You can change the time or turn it off at any time, or turn off notifications for the app in iOS Settings.
*   **How do I change the language of the app and the word meanings?**
    *   Solo Learning follows the language you choose for it in iOS: open **Settings > Apps > Solo Learning > Language**. An in-app language picker is planned.
*   **Is my progress saved to the cloud?**
    *   Not yet. Your progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move to a new phone automatically. Cloud sync is something I would like to add later.
*   **How do I start over?**
    *   Open **Profile** and choose **Reset game**. This clears your level, coins, items and streak.

## Troubleshooting

If you encounter any issues while using Solo Learning:

*   **Check for Updates:** Ensure your app is up to date in the App Store, as updates often contain important fixes, new content, and improved gameplay.
*   **Restart the App:** If updating doesn't resolve the issue, try restarting the app. If you experience a crash, please report the bug so I can investigate!
*   **Check Your Internet Connection:** The Inspirational Refreshments feature (a quote after a defeat) relies on an internet connection to retrieve quotes. Everything else works offline, and the app shows a Korean phrase instead when no quote can be loaded.
*   **No Sound?** Check the Music and Sound effects switches in **Profile**, your volume, and that your device isn't in a silent mode that you want to override. Korean words play even with the silent switch on.
*   **Words Show in the Wrong Language?** The app follows the language set for it in iOS Settings (see the FAQ above).
